What this is

Buy-and-hold of this week's PICK list over trailing prices. Ratings are not walked forward — a name that is a PICK today is treated as a pick on every day in the window. No costs, no rebalance. Cash earns 0 in the published book. Default book is the model portfolio, weights renormalized to 100% invested. Cash is a separate toggle. Benchmark is SPY.

Not a scoring-model backtest. A name that is a PICK on 2026-10-30 is treated as a pick on every day in the window. That embeds hindsight. Use this to see whether the current book is a momentum pile-up or a lagged recovery — not to claim the five-factor composite had edge historically. No commissions, no slippage, daily close-to-close.

Method

Alignment. Daily closes (CBOE delayed history) via hist.json, inner-joined to SPY and every pick with a full tape (260 sessions, 2025-10-20 to 2026-10-30). Weights. Model = inverse-vol sleeves with a 12% cap, renormalized to fully invested. Published keeps the 40.9% cash sleeve at a 0% return. Sharpe. Daily mean annualized minus 4%, divided by annualized daily vol. Next. Once Railway has more than one daily snapshot, this page can walk ratings forward instead of freezing this week's list.
